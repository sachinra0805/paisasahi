How we make money — in plain English

When you get approved for a card or loan through our link, the bank pays us a commission — typically ₹1,000–3,000. It costs you nothing extra.

The part that matters: that commission never changes a ranking. Our formula is published, payouts are disclosed, and if a high-paying product scores badly, it ranks badly. That's the whole business model — trust that compounds.
