The formula we use:
Net worth = Σ assets − Σ liabilities  ·  Debt-to-assets = liabilities ÷ assets × 100
Use resale values for things, outstanding balances for loans — both as of today, not what you paid or borrowed originally.
Your numbers stay yours: this page runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is sent or saved anywhere. Close the tab and it's gone. Value gold and property at what they would realistically sell for, not the sentimental or asking price.

Why one number beats ten calculators

A salary tells you what flows in. An EMI tells you what flows out. Neither tells you whether you're actually getting anywhere. Net worth does: if it's rising year after year, your money system works — whatever your income. If it's flat while income rises, something is leaking, and the balance sheet shows you where.

How to read the own-vs-owe bar

The bar splits everything you own into the part that's truly yours and the part lenders have a claim on. A young family with a fresh home loan might be at 20% own / 80% owe — normal. What matters is the direction: every EMI paid, every debt cleared, every rupee invested moves the green boundary right. Under 30% debt-to-assets is comfortable for most families; above 60% deserves a plan — start with the Debt Avalanche view.

Common questions

What is net worth?

Everything you own minus everything you owe. It's the single scoreboard number for your money — income is just one of the flows that changes it.

Should I count my home if I have a home loan?

Yes — count the home at realistic resale value as an asset and the outstanding loan as a liability. The gap between them is your equity. Counting only one side distorts the picture.

Is my PF / EPF an asset?

Yes. It's your money, even if withdrawal is restricted. Use the current passbook balance. Same for NPS and other retirement accounts.

How often should I redo this?

Twice a year is plenty. Net worth moves slowly; checking it monthly mostly measures market noise, not progress.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The calculation happens in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or tracked — that's rather the point of this site.
