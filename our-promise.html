Our Promise

Our promise, in writing

Published: October 2026 · Permanent

The short answer: three promises, public and permanent. (1) No spam, ever — we never sell your data to lenders or anyone else. (2) No bought rankings — a published formula decides every order; payouts can't move it. (3) Show our work — formulas, assumptions, and corrections all happen in public. This page is the receipt. Hold us to it.

Promise 1: No spam, ever

Here's how the typical Indian money site works: the "free credit score" or "free eligibility check" is bait. The moment you enter your phone number, it becomes the product — sold to lenders whose agents call you 20+ times a day. Their business isn't advice. It's your data.

Ours isn't. Our calculators work without sign-up — there's no form standing between you and the answer, because a form is where the spam begins. We don't sell, rent, or share your personal data with lenders, marketers, or anyone else, at any price. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you rights over your data; we go further than it requires by simply not collecting what we'd have to protect. (The law itself lives with MeitY if you want to read it.)

In practice: no "enter your mobile number to see results." No partner calls. No WhatsApp spam. If you ever get a sales call because of us, we've broken promise #1 — tell the world.

Promise 2: No bought rankings

Every "best credit cards" list you've seen was probably ordered by who pays the site the most. The mechanism is simple: hide the formula, rank the highest payer first, collect the commission. You can't prove it, because there's nothing to check.

We removed the hiding place. Our ranking methodology is published: every major option gets listed (including issuers that pay us nothing), every option is scored by the same formula on the same profiles, and what each issuer pays us is disclosed next to the results. A payout can never move a product up. If a high-paying card scores badly, it ranks badly — visibly, embarrassingly, permanently.

Promise 3: Show our work

Every ranking links to its formula. Every calculator shows its math and assumptions. Every guide carries an update date. When we're wrong — and we will be, sometimes — the correction goes on the page with a date, not into a memory hole. Trust isn't a tagline; it's a paper trail.

We use RBI and SEBI terminology for product definitions, cite sources on factual claims, and label every illustrative example as illustrative. If a number on this site can't be traced to a source or a shown calculation, that's a bug — report it.

How do you hold us to this?

  • This page is dated and permanent. If we ever quietly edit a promise, the edit history is the story.
  • The methodology page is checkable. Re-run any ranking's formula yourself. If the order doesn't follow the math, we've broken promise #2.
  • The money page is plain. How we make money is written in plain English, not legalese. If our revenue ever comes from something not on that page, we've broken the deal.

Promises 1 and 2 are what make us different. Promise 3 is what makes 1 and 2 believable.

Questions, answered

What happens if you break a promise?

Then we don't deserve your trust — and this page, dated and public, is the receipt you'd hold up. There's no fine print that overrides it.

Do you sell my email or phone number?

No. Not to lenders, not to marketers, not to anyone, at any price. Our tools work without sign-up precisely so there's nothing to sell.

How is this different from other Indian money sites?

Most are lead-generation businesses: the free score is bait and your number is the product. We're a publishing business: affiliate commissions when you're approved, disclosed plainly, and your data is never for sale.
