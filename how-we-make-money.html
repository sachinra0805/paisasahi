How we make money — in plain English

Published: October 2026 · Permanent

The short answer: when you're approved for a card or loan through our link, the bank pays us a commission — typically ₹1,000–3,000 per approval. It costs you nothing extra. It never changes our rankings. And we will never sell your data. That's the whole model, and this page is the permanent record of it.

Where does the money come from?

One source today: affiliate commissions. Banks and issuers pay publishers for approved customers — it's their customer-acquisition budget, the same money they'd spend on ads. When you click our link, apply, get approved, and activate, they pay us. Typical payouts in India: roughly ₹1,000–3,000 per approved credit card, more for premium cards and loans. The exact payout per product is disclosed next to our rankings.

What does it cost you?

Nothing. The commission comes out of the issuer's marketing budget, not your pocket. Same card, same fees, same terms as applying directly — the price to you is identical whether you use our link or not. Using it is how you fund the free calculators.

What's the firewall between money and rankings?

This is the part that matters. Our ranking methodology computes every order from a published formula before payouts enter the picture. Payouts are then disclosed next to the results — and they cannot move anything. A card that pays us ₹3,000 but scores badly ranks below a card that pays us nothing and scores well. If a payout could change the order, the page would be labelled advertising. That's the rule, in writing, on our promise page.

What will you add later?

As the site grows, two more revenue lines — both held to the same standard:

  • Display ads — clearly labelled, never disguised as content, never from lenders buying placement in rankings.
  • Our own tools — paid extras like a guided CIBIL-action toolkit or a tax-filing checklist. Built by us, priced plainly, refundable.

What will you never do?

  • Never sell your data. Not to lenders, not to marketers, not to anyone, at any price. Promise #1.
  • Never sell rankings. No paid placements, no "sponsored #1 pick." Promise #2.
  • Never spam you. No sales calls, no WhatsApp blasts, no "limited offer" panic.
  • Never pretend to be regulated. We're not a bank, broker, or SEBI-registered adviser — banks answer to the RBI and advisers to SEBI. We're a publisher. Our content is education, not financial advice.

If our revenue ever comes from something not listed on this page, we've broken the deal — and this dated page is the proof.

Questions, answered

Does it cost me extra to use your link?

No. The commission comes from the issuer's marketing budget. Same fees, same terms, same card as applying directly.

Do higher-paying products rank higher?

No. Rankings are computed by the published formula first; payouts are disclosed alongside and cannot change the order.

Will you ever sell my data?

No — permanently off the table. Our calculators work without sign-up, so there's minimal data to protect in the first place.

Are you a bank, broker, or investment adviser?

None of the above. PaisaSahi is an education website, not SEBI-registered and not a bank. Content is information, not financial advice.
